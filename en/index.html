The approach

Start with the need.
Not the technology.

AI is not an end in itself. The goal is to choose an approach that fits your data, your constraints and what you want to achieve.

  1. 01

    Understand

    The business context, the available data and what success would look like.

  2. 02

    Experiment

    Test promising approaches and check initial assumptions against the data.

  3. 03

    Evaluate

    Measure results, identify limitations and make evidence-based decisions.

  4. 04

    Integrate

    Connect the solution to your tools and hand over what you need to use it.
